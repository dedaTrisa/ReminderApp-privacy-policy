ReminderApp - 隐私政策 (中文)

应用信息

应用名称: ReminderApp
开发者: Vladimir Gavranic

数据收集与使用

您输入的提醒内容（文字、日期和时间）仅保存在您设备上应用的私有存储空间中。本应用不联网，没有账户，不使用云同步、统计分析或广告。我们不会收集、传输或与任何人共享您的数据。

权限说明

本应用申请以下权限，仅用于提醒功能：通知（用于显示闹钟）、振动、精确闹钟（用于准时响铃），以及手机重启后自动启动（用于重新设置您的闹钟）。不申请其他任何权限。

导出 CSV 与分享

“Export CSV”（导出 CSV）会在应用私有存储空间中生成文件。如果您点击“Share”（分享），CSV 内容将交给您自己选择的应用（例如电子邮件），去向完全由您决定，开发者不会收到任何内容。

数据主体权利

您可以在应用中直接删除单条提醒、历史记录或全部数据（More → Delete all data）。卸载应用将永久删除所有本地数据。

免责声明

闹钟取决于您手机的设置（已开启通知、电池限制等）。本应用无法保证在每台设备上都能响铃。

联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 gavranic76@gmail.com 联系我们。

ReminderApp - Privacy Policy (English)

App Information

App name: ReminderApp
Developer: Vladimir Gavranic

Data Collection and Usage

The reminders you enter (text, date and time) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app does not use the internet and has no accounts, cloud syncing, analytics or advertising. We do not collect, transmit or share your data with anyone.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions, used only for the reminder function: notifications (to show the alarm), vibration, exact alarms (to ring at the exact minute), and starting after the phone restarts (to set your alarms again). No other permissions are requested.

Export CSV and Share

"Export CSV" creates a file in the app's private storage. If you tap "Share", the CSV content is handed to the app you choose (for example e-mail). Only you decide where it goes; the developer receives nothing.

Data Subject Rights

You can delete single reminders, the history, or all data directly in the app (More → Delete all data). Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.

Disclaimer

Alarms depend on your phone settings (notifications on, battery restrictions). The app cannot guarantee that an alarm will ring on every device.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: gavranic76@gmail.com
